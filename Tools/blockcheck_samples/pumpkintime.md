Seeing the pumpkins outside Trader Joe's took me straight back to my student days. When I was doing my master's, I used to go there regularly to stock up on groceries. I loved the stuff we got at Trader Joe's, and I still do. The vibe was great, and the staff were always so friendly. The store I used to go to in Boston was the smallest Trader Joe's in the world. 

<insert image IMG_3919.HEIC "From the Boston store at 899 Boylston Street" place="Boston, Massachusetts">

During Halloween and pumpkin season, they sold all kinds of pumpkin-flavoured snacks, and there used to be huge lines outside. Because the store was so small, they only let in a few people at a time, so we would wait outside in the cold, and somehow that became part of the fun.

## Student meals

I used to get pasta and pasta sauce there. It was the best place to get tasty ingredients for less, and as a student, that makes all the difference. I also used to make jalapeño cheese sandwiches that were so good: pepper jack cheese, jalapeños, multigrain bread and a drizzle of olive oil. It was my own recipe, and I ate it for almost a year. It's funny how a sandwich that simple can still be one of the meals I remember most.

I love the Trader Joe's Caesar salad kit and all the small snacks they have, and their yoghurts are amazing too. Going to Trader Joe's always takes me back to being a student, counting every cent and making simple meals. Life was tighter back then, but it was also simpler. Every trip felt like a small treat, and I didn't realise at the time how much I would miss those days. Now, walking past a stack of pumpkins at the door is enough to bring all of it back: the cold Boston air, the long line, and the excitement of finding something new on the shelves.

There isn't a Trader Joe's near my house in Seattle, so I was happy to find that this one in Monterey is right on my way back from work. I'm looking forward to stopping by often for yummy snacks, and for a little taste of my student days every time I do.
