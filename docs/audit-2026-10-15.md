Rules: `docs/design.md` -> "Consistency rules". Looks and flow only; no behaviour, data or network changes.

## Not changed (with reasons)
- Splash tile radius 33: mirrors the app-icon mask; a token would make it wrong.
- Lamp swatch / kelvin literal colours: they are data being sent to the lamps.
- Fixed `.font(.system(size:))` on symbols in fixed tiles (22/26/34 pt): intentional, documented as allowed.
- Status service rows / info cards use control radius 20 surfaces while hero uses 28: intentional two-level card system, now written down.
- Fan "unavailable" has no fix action: it is a Pi-side install, nothing the app can do.
- Plain `.buttonStyle(.plain)` chips in Lights use glass directly instead of `.secondaryAction`: they are tiles, not full-width buttons.
- Frames/*: owned by the other agent. Observed there (for them): `.red` in ~12 places (use `Theme.danger`), `.font(.caption)` ad-hoc fonts, `padding(.bottom, 24)` in StoryEditorView, "..." ellipses; `Theme.danger`, `DS.Space.hair/tabBar` and `AdaptiveStack` exist on this branch only.
